Overview

The AI-101 continuously monitors the incoming single-phase supply and automatically disconnects the motor the moment an unsafe condition is detected — protecting the pump winding from the voltage swings, dry-running and overload events that cause most premature motor failures in the field. Once the supply returns to a safe range, the panel restarts the motor on its own, so households and small farms get dependable, largely unattended pump operation.

Its compact single-phase enclosure and straightforward wiring make it a popular choice wherever a simple, reliable upgrade over a basic manual starter switch is needed.

Key Features

  • Low / High Voltage Protection
  • Phase Failure Protection
  • Electronic Overload & Dry Run Protection
  • Auto Start Facility
  • Single Phasing & OFF Timer Facility

Typical Applications

  • Domestic borewell & overhead tank pumps
  • Small agricultural submersible pump sets
  • Shops & small commercial single-phase pump loads
