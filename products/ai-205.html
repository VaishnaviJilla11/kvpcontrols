Electronic Control Panel

Model: AI-205

A compact three-phase electronic control panel with a digital volt/amp display, built for everyday submersible pump duty.

Overview

The AI-205 watches incoming three-phase line voltage and current in real time, cutting power to the motor the instant it sees high voltage, overload, dry-run or single-phasing conditions. The onboard digital display lets an installer or farmer glance at live volt/amp readings for quick troubleshooting, while the built-in OFF timer facility allows scheduled shutdown without a separate time switch.

It is sized to drop into standard three-phase submersible installations without adding extra panel bulk, making it a straightforward, cost-effective protection upgrade.

Key Features

  • High Voltage & Overload Protection
  • Dryrun & Single Phasing Protection
  • OFF Timer Facility
  • Digital Volt / Amp Display

Typical Applications

  • Agricultural submersible pump sets
  • Small-to-medium industrial three-phase motors
  • Three-phase water supply schemes

English: "OL" means the motor current has exceeded the set overload limit — check the motor/load for jamming or overheating before resuming. "drY" means the panel detected no-load/dry-run conditions — check the pump/water source before restarting.

తెలుగు: "OL" అంటే మోటారు కరెంట్ సెట్ చేసిన ఓవర్‌లోడ్ పరిమితిని మించిపోయింది — మళ్లీ ఆన్ చేసే ముందు మోటారు/లోడ్‌లో జామింగ్ లేదా వేడెక్కడం లేదని చూసుకోండి. "drY" అంటే ప్యానెల్ నో-లోడ్/డ్రై-రన్ పరిస్థితిని గుర్తించింది — మళ్లీ ఆన్ చేసే ముందు పంప్/నీటి మూలాన్ని తనిఖీ చేయండి.
