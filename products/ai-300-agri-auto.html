Overview

Rural power supplies often bring phase failures, voltage swings and dry borewells — the AI-300 Agri Auto combines automatic phase-sequence protection, dry-run/no-load protection and an adjustable overload relay in one panel, so farmers get worry-free, largely automatic pump operation even when the incoming feeder quality is inconsistent.

Sized for motors up to 7.5 HP, it is built to cope with the dust, heat and voltage fluctuations typical of agricultural installations, and is backed by a 1-year warranty for added peace of mind in the field.

Key Features

  • Automatic Phase Preventer
  • Dry-Run / No-Load Protection
  • Overload Protection
  • 1-Year Warranty

Typical Applications

  • Borewell & open-well agricultural pump sets
  • Drip & sprinkler irrigation pump houses
  • Farm water-lifting stations up to 7.5 HP
