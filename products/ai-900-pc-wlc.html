Overview

The AI-900 PC-WLC's automatic phase changer logic continuously checks the incoming three-phase supply and selects the healthiest phase combination on its own — removing the need for anyone to manually correct a reversed or faulty phase sequence at the panel. Its integrated water level controller (WLC) senses sump and overhead tank levels and starts or stops the pump automatically, so the tank fills without overflowing and the pump never runs dry because the source has emptied.

On top of that tank-management intelligence, it carries the same high/low voltage, overload and dry-run protection found across the AI series, giving tank-fed systems a single panel that handles both motor protection and water-level automation.

Key Features

  • Automatic Phase Change Facility
  • WLC Water Level Controller
  • High/Low Voltage, Overload & Dry Run Protection
  • Heavy Duty & Reliable Performance
  • One Touch Operation

Typical Applications

  • Overhead tank filling systems
  • Housing societies, hostels & schools with storage tanks
