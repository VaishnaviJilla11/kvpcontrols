Star-Delta Starter

Semi-Automatic · 1 to 15 HP

A controlled start for three-phase pump and motor applications — starting in star and changing to delta for normal running.

Overview

A star-delta starter reduces the current drawn at start-up compared to a direct-on-line start by first energising the motor windings in star configuration — where each winding sees a lower voltage — and then switching to delta for full-voltage normal running once the motor is close to running speed. This makes it a good fit for larger motors where a straight DOL start would otherwise draw an excessive inrush current from the supply.

KVP's unit is built from a line contactor, a star contactor, a delta contactor and an overload relay, and is available in semi-automatic or fully-automatic changeover. It requires a six-terminal motor that is rated to run in delta.

Key Features

  • Reduces Starting Current vs. D.O.L.
  • Line, Star & Delta Contactors + Overload Relay
  • Needs a Six-Terminal Motor Rated for Delta
  • Semi-Automatic or Fully-Automatic Changeover

Typical Applications

  • Agricultural & industrial pump motors from 1 to 15 HP
  • Deep borewell submersible motors
  • Compressors & machinery needing reduced starting current
